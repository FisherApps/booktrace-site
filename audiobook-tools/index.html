About these tools

Useful whether you are planning one book or a whole listening life.

Do these tools connect to Audible or Libby?

No. Enter the runtime or details shown in your audiobook app. The tools work without access to your private account history.

Are the audiobook calculators free?

Yes. The speed and listening-goal calculators are free to use in your browser, with no Booktrace account required.

What is Booktrace?

Booktrace is an iPhone audiobook tracker for sessions, chapters, narrators, playback speed, notes, and listening statistics.

Can I use Booktrace with multiple audiobook apps?

Yes. Booktrace is a companion tracker, so you can keep one listening history across Audible, Libby, Spotify, Apple Books, Everand, and more.
