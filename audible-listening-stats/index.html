The boundary

Every player sees its own room. You listen across the whole house.

Listening statistics inside a service are useful and accurate for that service. They cannot automatically become a durable, cross-service record of every audiobook you heard elsewhere.

Two different kinds of stats

Account activity versus a personal listening history.

Service statistics
  • Activity recorded inside one player or account
  • Useful for seeing how you use that service
  • May not follow you when you borrow or buy elsewhere
Booktrace statistics
  • Sessions you choose to record from any source
  • Books, narrators, chapters, speed, and actual time together
  • A private history that remains independent of the player

What Booktrace keeps

The books behind the number.

Total hours are more meaningful when you can see which covers filled the month, which days you listened, how many sessions it took, and which narrator was in your headphones.

Booktrace connects the summary back to every audiobook and session instead of leaving it as an isolated lifetime counter.

What Booktrace does not do

No pretend Audible integration.

Booktrace does not ask for your Audible password, read your Audible account, or automatically import its private listening history. The same is true for Libby, Spotify, Apple Books, and other players.

Questions, answered

Audible stats and cross-app tracking.

Where are Audible listening stats?

Audible provides them inside its app. Navigation changes over time, so check the profile or listening-history area in your installed version.

Do they include Libby or Spotify?

No. Statistics inside one service describe activity recorded by that service.

Can Booktrace import Audible history?

No. It does not sign in to or import private account history from Audible or other players.

Can I add past listening?

Yes. Record a known past session or run the live listening screen during a new one.

Does playback speed count?

Yes. A session can include speed so actual time and story progress remain separate.

Where is Booktrace data stored?

Your library and listening history stay on your iPhone. No Booktrace account is required.
