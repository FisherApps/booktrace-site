Common questions

Series and box sets, explained

How do I calculate total time for a series?

Add up every book's runtime, then divide by your playback speed to get total listening time.

Does speed apply to the whole series?

Yes, one speed applies across every book here. Calculate remaining books separately if your speed changes partway through.

Can I use this for a box set?

Yes. Treat each disc, book, or part as a row and add as many as you need.

How is the finish date calculated?

Total adjusted listening time divided by your daily minutes, rounded up to whole days, added to today.
