The simple math

Words ÷ clip length = words per minute

A narrator speaking 155 words in one minute reads at 155 wpm. If you prefer listening around 210 wpm, playing that narrator at roughly 1.35× gets you there.

This is why the same playback speed can feel too slow for one narrator and too fast for another — their natural pace, not just your speed setting, sets the actual words per minute you hear.

Common questions

Narration pace, explained

How do I find a narrator's words per minute?

Count or estimate the words spoken in a short clip, then divide by the clip's length in minutes.

What is a normal narration speed?

Most professional narrators speak between 150 and 160 words per minute at normal, 1× playback.

How do I pick a playback speed from this?

Enter the pace you find comfortable, and the calculator suggests the speed that reaches it from this narrator's natural pace.

Does pace vary by narrator?

Yes, significantly. Some read closer to 140 wpm and others closer to 175, which is why one playback speed doesn't fit every book.
