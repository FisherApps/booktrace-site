The simple math

Price ÷ runtime in hours

A $30, 25-hour audiobook costs $1.20 an hour. A $10, 3-hour audiobook costs $3.33 an hour. Price alone would suggest the shorter book is the deal — cost per hour says the opposite.

This compares published runtime, not your speed-adjusted listening time. Pair it with the speed calculator if you want value per hour you'll actually spend listening.

Common questions

Cost per hour, explained

How do I calculate cost per hour?

Divide the price paid by the runtime in hours.

Why not just compare price?

A longer, pricier audiobook can cost less per hour than a shorter, cheaper one, so cost per hour is the fairer comparison.

Can I compare a credit to buying a book?

Yes. Enter what the credit effectively costs you — monthly price divided by credits — as the price for that option.

Does this account for playback speed?

No, it uses published runtime. Use the speed calculator alongside it for listening-time-adjusted value.
