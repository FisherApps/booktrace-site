How long is the Atomic Habits audiobook?

Atomic Habits by James Clear narrated by James Clear is about 5 hours and 35 minutes long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 3 hr 43 min, and at 2× to about 2 hr 48 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.

At one hour a day and normal speed, you'll finish in about 6 days. Speed it up to 1.5× and the same hour a day finishes it in about 4 days. At 45 minutes a day and 1.5×, plan on about 5 days.
