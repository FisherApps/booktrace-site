Common questions

Hunger Games audiobook series length, answered

How long are all the Hunger Games audiobooks?

All 3 Hunger Games audiobooks in the Tatiana Maslany recording total about 33 hours and 37 minutes at normal speed.

How long does it take to listen to the whole Hunger Games series at 1.5x?

At 1.5x speed the full series takes about 22 hours and 25 minutes. At 2x it takes about 16 hours and 49 minutes.

How many days to finish the Hunger Games audiobook series?

At one hour a day and normal speed it takes about 34 days. At 1.5x and an hour a day, about 23 days.

Which Hunger Games audiobook is the longest?

The Hunger Games is the longest at about 11 hours and 37 minutes. Catching Fire is the shortest at about 11 hours.
