How long is the The Midnight Library audiobook?

The Midnight Library by Matt Haig narrated by Carey Mulligan is about 8 hours and 50 minutes long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 5 hr 53 min, and at 2× to about 4 hr 25 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.
