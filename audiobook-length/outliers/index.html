How long is the Outliers audiobook?

Outliers by Malcolm Gladwell narrated by Malcolm Gladwell is about 7 hours long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 4 hr 40 min, and at 2× to about 3 hr 30 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.

How many days to finish Outliers?

At one hour a day and normal speed, you'll finish in about 7 days. Speed it up to 1.5× and the same hour a day finishes it in about 5 days. At 45 minutes a day and 1.5×, plan on about 7 days.

Outliers audiobook length, answered

How long is the Outliers audiobook?

The Outliers audiobook by Malcolm Gladwell runs about 7 hours at normal (1x) speed, in the edition narrated by Malcolm Gladwell. Exact runtime can differ by a few minutes between editions and stores.

How long is Outliers at 1.5x speed?

At 1.5x speed, Outliers takes about 4 hours and 40 minutes. At 2x it takes about 3 hours and 30 minutes.

How many days does it take to listen to Outliers?

At one hour a day and normal speed, Outliers takes about 7 days. At 1.5x speed and an hour a day it takes about 5 days. At 30 minutes a day and normal speed it takes about 14 days.
