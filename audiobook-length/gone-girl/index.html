How long is the Gone Girl audiobook?

Gone Girl by Gillian Flynn narrated by Julia Whelan and Kirby Heyborne is about 19 hours and 1 minutes long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 12 hr 41 min, and at 2× to about 9 hr 31 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.
