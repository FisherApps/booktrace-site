How long is the To Kill a Mockingbird audiobook?

To Kill a Mockingbird by Harper Lee narrated by Sissy Spacek is about 12 hours and 15 minutes long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 8 hr 10 min, and at 2× to about 6 hr 8 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.

How many days to finish To Kill a Mockingbird?

At one hour a day and normal speed, you'll finish in about 13 days. Speed it up to 1.5× and the same hour a day finishes it in about 9 days. At 45 minutes a day and 1.5×, plan on about 11 days.
