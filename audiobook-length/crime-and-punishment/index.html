How long is the Crime and Punishment audiobook?

Crime and Punishment by Fyodor Dostoevsky is about 21 hours long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 14 hr, and at 2× to about 10 hr 30 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.

How many days to finish Crime and Punishment?

At one hour a day and normal speed, you'll finish in about 21 days. Speed it up to 1.5× and the same hour a day finishes it in about 14 days. At 45 minutes a day and 1.5×, plan on about 19 days.
