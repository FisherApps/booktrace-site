How long is the Thinking, Fast and Slow audiobook?

Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman narrated by Patrick Egan is about 20 hours and 2 minutes long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 13 hr 21 min, and at 2× to about 10 hr 1 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.

How many days to finish Thinking, Fast and Slow?

At one hour a day and normal speed, you'll finish in about 21 days. Speed it up to 1.5× and the same hour a day finishes it in about 14 days. At 45 minutes a day and 1.5×, plan on about 18 days.

How long, really

20 hr 2 min in perspective

At 20 hr 2 min, Thinking, Fast and Slow is roughly a full day of non-stop listening, a road trip from Seattle to Denver. That's longer than about 70% of the 65 popular audiobooks we list.
