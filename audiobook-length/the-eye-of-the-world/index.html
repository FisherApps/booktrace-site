How long is the The Eye of the World audiobook?

The Eye of the World by Robert Jordan narrated by Michael Kramer and Kate Reading is about 29 hours long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 19 hr 20 min, and at 2× to about 14 hr 30 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.
