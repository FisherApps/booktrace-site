How long is the Les Misérables audiobook?

Les Misérables by Victor Hugo is about 60 hours long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 40 hr, and at 2× to about 30 hr.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.

How many days to finish Les Misérables?

At one hour a day and normal speed, you'll finish in about 60 days. Speed it up to 1.5× and the same hour a day finishes it in about 40 days. At 45 minutes a day and 1.5×, plan on about 54 days.
