How long is the The Fellowship of the Ring audiobook?

The Fellowship of the Ring by J.R.R. Tolkien narrated by Rob Inglis is about 19 hours long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 12 hr 40 min, and at 2× to about 9 hr 30 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.
