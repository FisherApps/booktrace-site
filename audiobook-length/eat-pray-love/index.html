How long is the Eat Pray Love audiobook?

Eat Pray Love by Elizabeth Gilbert narrated by Elizabeth Gilbert is about 13 hours long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 8 hr 40 min, and at 2× to about 6 hr 30 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.

How many days to finish Eat Pray Love?

At one hour a day and normal speed, you'll finish in about 13 days. Speed it up to 1.5× and the same hour a day finishes it in about 9 days. At 45 minutes a day and 1.5×, plan on about 12 days.
