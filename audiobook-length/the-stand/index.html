How long is the The Stand audiobook?

The Stand by Stephen King narrated by Grover Gardner is about 47 hours long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 31 hr 20 min, and at 2× to about 23 hr 30 min.

Runtimes are approximate and vary slightly by edition and retailer. Check the length shown in Audible, Libby, Spotify or Apple Books for your copy.
