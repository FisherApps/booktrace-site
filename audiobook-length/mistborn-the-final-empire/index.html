How long is the Mistborn: The Final Empire audiobook?

Mistborn: The Final Empire by Brandon Sanderson narrated by Michael Kramer is about 24 hours and 38 minutes long at normal speed. At 1.5× that drops to about 16 hr 25 min, and at 2× to about 12 hr 19 min.
