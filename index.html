Common questions

Booktrace, explained

Does it work for print books and ebooks, or only audiobooks?

All three, equally. For a print book or ebook you say which page you’re on and Booktrace counts the pages down; for an audiobook it tracks chapters, narrator, and speed. Every format gets sessions, time, streaks, goals, and stats.

What is an audiobook tracker?

A tool that records what you're listening to, your progress by chapter, and the time you've actually spent listening. Booktrace does that—and tracks your print books and ebooks by page in the same library.

Is Booktrace an app or a printable tracker?

Booktrace is an iPhone app. Start a session with one tap or log one afterward, and it keeps your page or chapter, time, and stats instead of a journal or spreadsheet.

Does it track Audible, Libby, and Spotify audiobooks?

Yes—Booktrace keeps one listening history across Audible, Libby, Spotify, Apple Books, Everand, and other audiobook apps.

Can I import from Goodreads or Libby?

Yes. Import a Goodreads or Libby export and your shelves, ratings, finish dates, formats, and page counts come along.

Does Booktrace require an account?

No. Your library, notes, and reading history stay on your iPhone—no account and no public reading profile.
