The simple math

Total monthly cost ÷ audiobooks used

A membership only pays off compared with its included credits. Anything you listen to beyond those credits gets priced at your individual rate, and anything you skip does not count as a saving here.

If you regularly use fewer credits than you're charged for, buying individually — or borrowing from a library app — usually wins on a per-book basis.

Common questions

Membership vs. buying, explained

How do I know if a membership is worth it?

Compare the total monthly cost — including anything bought beyond your credits — against buying every audiobook individually.

What if I don't use all my credits?

Enter only the audiobooks you expect to finish. Unused credits don't count as savings until you actually redeem them.

Does this work for Audible, Chirp, or other services?

Yes. Enter any membership price and credit count — it doesn't need to know which service you use.

What about books bought beyond my credits?

Add them to "audiobooks you'll use." They're priced at your individual rate automatically.
